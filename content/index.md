I’m **Jonas**. a computer science student, lifelong learner, and maker of things.

This space is my **digital garden** a place where ideas grow over time, connected through links and enriched with my observations.


I don’t follow the Zettelkasten method or any rigid note-taking system.  
I write notes to think, summarize what I learn, and connect ideas using backlinks when they feel related.  
Some notes are rough, some are refined



### About this site

This garden is built using **[Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/)**, with content written in **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)** and published as a static site.

If something feels unfinished, that’s intentional.  
Learning is never done.
